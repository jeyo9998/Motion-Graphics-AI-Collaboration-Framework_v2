## Raw response

## Motion Principles Specification

- **Objective:** A single title fades in while rising into its final position, then holds still for the remainder of a 3-second composition.
- **User requirements (verbatim intent):**
  - Composition: 1920×1080, 30 fps.
  - Duration: 3 seconds (90 frames at 30 fps).
  - Motion: fade in **while** moving upward by **about 120 pixels**.
  - Motion window: the **first second** of the composition.
  - After that: hold still for the rest of the composition.
  - Output must be application-neutral (implementable in Alight Motion or After Effects).
- **Recommendations (clearly labelled):**
  - *(Proposal)* Let opacity reach 100% slightly before the travel completes (~0.7–0.8 s), so the title is readable while it is still settling.
  - *(Proposal)* Use a single shared easing curve for both opacity and position, so the fade and the move read as one gesture rather than two.
  - *(Proposal)* Keep the title fully transparent at frame 0 — no visible flash or ghost before the fade begins.
- **Subject and role:** The title is the only animated element and the sole point of attention for the whole 3 seconds. Nothing else in the composition is required to animate, enter, or exit.
- **Spatial path:** Vertical only. The title travels upward along a straight line, parallel to the frame's vertical axis, covering **about 120 px** (approximate, as stated) between its starting position and its final resting position. No horizontal travel, arc, or curve. The starting position is the resting position offset downward by ~120 px, so the final resting position is the composition's anchor for the title's layout.
- **Rotation / scale / opacity:**
  - Rotation: none (no rotation is requested).
  - Scale: none (no scaling is requested).
  - Opacity: 0% → 100% across the movement window, so the title is invisible before its fade and fully opaque afterwards.
- **Timing table** (times are composition time; frame numbers assume frame 0 = t 0, so t 1.0 s = frame 30, t 3.0 s = frame 90):

| Property | Start | End | Value | Exact / approx | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical position (Y offset from resting position) | 0.0 s (frame 0) | 1.0 s (frame 30) | +120 px → 0 px (offset downward → resting) | Approximate distance (user said "about 120 px"); exact start/end times | Upward travel only; final resting position offset is relative, so the title's layout anchor is unchanged |
| Opacity | 0.0 s (frame 0) | 1.0 s (frame 30) | 0% → 100% | Exact values; exact times per the "first second" requirement | *(Proposal)* Could end ~0.7–0.8 s instead, so text is readable while still settling |
| Horizontal position | 0.0 s | 3.0 s (frame 90) | Constant — no movement | Exact (no horizontal motion requested) | Static for the full duration |
| Scale / rotation | 0.0 s | 3.0 s | Constant — no change | Exact | Left at identity values |
| Hold | 1.0 s (frame 30) | 3.0 s (frame 90) | All animated values frozen at their end values | Exact | Title sits motionless for the final 2 seconds |

- **Easing character:**
  - **Movement:** decelerating ease-out — the title picks up speed quickly near the start and slows into its resting position, arriving without any overshoot, bounce, or rebound. Feel: an arrival that settles rather than stops abruptly. *(Proposal, not a fixed number)*: an ease-out of the family "roughly three quarters of the 120 px covered in the first third of the second"; the exact curve is an implementation choice.
  - **Opacity:** soft fade that rises promptly and finishes cleanly, so the text becomes readable well before the motion ends. *(Proposal)*: opacity completes slightly ahead of the travel, so the fade reads as "text arrives, then seats."
  - **Sync:** both properties begin at the same instant (t 0) and end within the same 1-second window; any difference between them should be a fraction of the window, not a separate animation.
  - Easing is described here by observable character only. Each application expresses curves in its own terms; exact curve names and parameter values are an application-level decision, and how a given app realizes "ease-out" or per-property curves needs verification in that app.
- **Composition and hierarchy:** 1920×1080 canvas, 16:9, 30 fps, 3.0 s / 90 frames total. The title is the single focal element and the entire visual hierarchy of the animation; no secondary elements, background changes, or typography changes are specified or implied. Typography, color, and the title's final resting position are **not specified by the brief** — this specification does not set them, and they should come from the existing layout the title belongs to. Final placement should keep the title fully within frame and legible once opaque.
- **Transitions:** One entry event — the title fades in and rises into place between 0.0 s and 1.0 s. No exit event within the 3-second duration; the title is still on screen at the final frame. No other layers enter, exit, or overlap. If the title shares the composition with other elements, their behavior is unspecified and should not be inferred from this specification.
- **Acceptance criteria (observable in a preview or render):**
  1. The title is fully transparent at t 0 and never shows a partial or residual frame before the fade begins.
  2. Opacity reaches 100% and remains at 100% from 1.0 s to the end of the composition.
  3. Vertical travel is upward, in a straight line, with no horizontal component, and covers approximately 120 px (tolerance ±5 px) between t 0 and t 1.0 s.
  4. The move completes at 1.0 s; no positional change is observable between 1.0 s and 3.0 s.
  5. Motion decelerates into place: there is no overshoot past the resting position, no bounce, and no visible stop-and-restart.
  6. The title rests at its intended layout position, fully legible, and entirely within the 1920×1080 frame.
  7. Scale and rotation are unchanged for the full duration.
  8. Total duration is exactly 3.0 s / 90 frames at 30 fps.
- **Dependencies and unknowns:**
  - Final resting position of the title in the frame: not specified; the spec treats movement as a relative offset so the existing layout anchor is preserved.
  - Whether the title is text, a graphic, or a group: not specified; the motion behaves identically in all cases.
  - Whether the fade should end slightly before the travel ends (proposal above) or simultaneously at 1.0 s: a creative choice for the user.
  - Whether the title should be on screen (but invisible) before t 0, or should not exist at all before t 0: minor, observable only in the first frame.
  - No source assets, fonts, or other elements were supplied, and none are assumed.
- **Verification required in native application:** Confirm in the target application that the opacity keyframe at t 0 is truly zero (no minimum-opacity floor), that the vertical offset resolves to the intended resting anchor after the move, that the ease-out character reads as a decelerating arrival rather than a linear slide, and that no extra keyframes or auto-generated handles were introduced on unrelated properties. Because the two target applications express curves, keyframes, and layer offsets with different controls and units, the mapping of this specification to each application's procedure is out of scope here and belongs to the application-adaptation step, which should treat the values in the timing table as the shared source of truth rather than re-deriving them per application.
