## User prompt

> Make my logo reveal feel more premium.

## Raw response

I can't tune this blind — "premium" is a relationship between timing, weight, and restraint in *your* reveal, and I don't have the logo, the duration, or a preview. I'd rather not invent flaws for you to fix.

**One thing to send me:** a short preview of the reveal (or 3–5 frames spread across it), plus how long it runs.

Once I can see it, the areas I'd most likely review — labelled as possibilities, not a diagnosis:

- **Easing character** — whether the arrival and settle read as deliberate rather than uniform.
- **Layering and stagger** — whether parts of the mark arrive as one block instead of with intentional separation.
- **The finish** — whether the last beat holds still enough, without excess flash, blur, or overshoot.

Color, typography, and effects stay off the list until there's material, so nothing is guessed. Other details (target application, brand constraints, exact durations) can wait for the preview rather than being answered now.

With material in hand, the sequence is: confirm what "premium" means for your brand and context, then produce a Motion Principles Specification (composition, hierarchy, timing table, easing character, acceptance criteria) that can be adapted to either target application without changing the intended feel.
